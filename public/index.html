Remixing is a digital movement that engages Creators, their fans and their fans' fans. Remix.Kred is the landing page for creators who remix.

Marzieh Shirali, a Showcase Creator on Collect.Kred, made the original and the Remix in the slider.

The Remix Protocol sets the rules for every Remix. A Collector collects a work, remixes a work they own, and the next Collector collects the Remix.

Collect»Remix»Collect

Every collect of a Remix rewards the original artist, the Remixer and the Platform. The original artist receives a third of every Remix collected.

A .Kred name with its Kredentials page costs $19 per year.

.Kred Identity for AI Agents

Kred identityKred ScoreAI agentsTokenization

Every AI agent needs a name and a reputation.

Each AI agent can hold its own .Kred name, the same as a person. The name holds the agent's identity, trust, skills, play, reputation, memory and Kred Score.

Every .Kred name mints a Kred Domain Token, an ERC-721 on Base. ENS anchors the identity record to Ethereum, so one name resolves across DNS, ENS and the ERC-8004 agent registry.
